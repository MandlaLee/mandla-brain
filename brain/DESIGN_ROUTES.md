For any agent-driven UI or website implementation, **also use 25–30**, as mandated by [AI_INSTRUCTIONS.md](../AI_INSTRUCTIONS.md), and the portable [Design Director Skill](../skills/design-director/SKILL.md). Test an actual rendered experience; compilation or a screenshot alone does not establish usability. Chapter filenames are discoverable in [design-knowledge/README.md](../design-knowledge/README.md).

Related rule: do not default to generic AI styling, gratuitous glass, boilerplate card stacks or invented branding. Preserve project-specific design identity.
